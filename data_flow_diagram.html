Data Store Dictionary

  • D1 Users — admin/staff credentials, roles, and session tokens
  • D2 Patients — personal details, contact info, medical history, allergies
  • D3 Consultations — visit records, chief complaint, findings, diagnosis, prescription, follow-up
  • D4 Pregnancies — EDD, OB index, status, delivery date
  • D5 Pregnancy Tracking Forms — monthly rows, trimester visit dates, document submission
  • D6 Reports — generated clinic summaries and analytics
  • D7 Audit Logs — timestamps and actions for accountability
